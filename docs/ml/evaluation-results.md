# Evaluation results and diagnostics

The [overview](README.md) gives the headline results. This page keeps the
aggregate metrics and diagnostic slices together without mixing historical
experiments with the current serving policy. Values are rounded from the linked
evaluation files. Lower market forecast errors are better.

The current model-selection validation comparison used 2,750 bank-feed and 44
manual in-scope examples per candidate. The committed
[selection record](../../data/evaluation/transaction_categories/classification_v2_model_selection.json)
stores the decision; `financial-ai-evaluate-semantic-classification` regenerates
the full metric report.

| Candidate and slice | Accuracy | Macro-F1 | Auto coverage | Accepted accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TF-IDF, bank | 88.7% | 88.6% | 96.5% | 90.0% |
| E5, bank | 79.3% | 79.2% | 80.5% | 90.0% |
| TF-IDF, manual | 56.8% | 55.2% | 50.0% | 90.9% |
| E5, manual | 95.5% | 95.4% | 100.0% | 95.5% |

On four out-of-scope manual validation examples, E5 rejected 4/4 and TF-IDF
rejected 3/4. The separate frozen
[manual final test](../../data/evaluation/transaction_categories/classification_v2_manual_test.json)
had 44 in-scope and four out-of-scope cases: 95.5% accuracy, 95.4% macro-F1,
97.7% auto coverage, 95.3% accepted accuracy, and 4/4 out-of-scope rejections.
The 22 known-concept/new-phrase cases scored 90.9% accuracy; the 22
novel-concept cases scored 100%. These small slices are descriptive.

The current bank-feed service accepted 879/1,500 generated transactions
(58.6%); all 879 labels matched the generator. This simulation checks serving
behavior, not accuracy on independent bank data. Its predecessor's
[selection record](../../data/evaluation/transaction_categories/classification_v2_bank_policy_selection.json)
reports 76.1% coverage at 97.9% accepted accuracy on controlled validation
and 62.8% at 93.7% on a pre-integration simulation. Those are historical
policy-selection results, not current-service metrics.

The full regenerated reports retain per-category precision, recall, F1,
support, confusion matrices, and other slices under
`data/runtime/ml/transaction_categories/`. The
[evaluation commands](reproducibility.md) show how to generate them.

not evaluated on the frozen manual final test.

## Volatility forecasting

The early baseline validation comparison evaluates transparent statistical
references against Ridge regression on outer validation (2022–2023). It is
generated by `financial-ai-evaluate-market-validation` under
`data/runtime/ml/market_forecast/evaluations/market_volatility_validation_us-large-cap-baselines-v1.json`
(also recorded in project history at `git show ade6d9a:docs/ml/market_volatility_baseline.md`):

| Validation baseline | MAE | RMSE | QLIKE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Train mean | 0.0869 | 0.1262 | 0.4802 |
| Train median | 0.0962 | 0.1418 | 0.8045 |
| 20-day persistence | 0.0814 | 0.1173 | 0.3510 |
| EWMA | 0.0738 | 0.1064 | 0.2647 |
| Ridge | 0.0667 | 0.1034 | 0.2747 |
| Ridge + EWMA feature | 0.0667 | 0.1034 | 0.2745 |

The checksum-linked [inner cross-validation record](../../data/evaluation/market_forecast/inner_cv_v1.summary.json)
ranked three declared XGBoost candidates by mean original-scale MAE:

| Candidate | Mean MAE | Mean RMSE | Mean QLIKE | Median boosting rounds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Flexible, selected | 0.08333 | 0.13738 | 0.76857 | 144 |
| Balanced | 0.08349 | 0.13722 | 0.77260 | 301 |
| Shallow | 0.08384 | 0.13763 | 0.77605 | 153 |

The selected candidate's fold MAE was 0.05169 in 2019, 0.14774 in 2020,
and 0.05056 in 2021. Selection used training-period folds; the later test was
not used to tune the model.

The [outer validation](../../data/evaluation/market_forecast/outer_validation_v1.summary.json)
and [final test](../../data/evaluation/market_forecast/final_test_v1.summary.json)
each report MAE, RMSE, and QLIKE for EWMA, Ridge, and XGBoost; the final test
also reports bias. Their complete aggregate tables are in the
[forecasting guide](volatility-forecasting.md). QLIKE uses the realized-to-
predicted variance ratio; bias is mean prediction minus realization.
